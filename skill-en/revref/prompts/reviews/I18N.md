# I18N — Internationalization

Finding ID prefix: I18N. Use together with `prompts/common-rules.md`.

Review internationalization and localization. Check:
- hardcoded strings in the UI, error messages, emails and notifications outside the translation system;
- phrases glued together from pieces, which break in other languages; pluralization (1 file / 2 files / 5 files);
- formatting of dates, times, numbers and currencies according to the user's locale and time zone;
- completeness and freshness of translations: missing, outdated and unused keys; behavior when a translation is missing;
- layout: text expansion after translation (up to 30–40%), RTL support, fonts for the required scripts;
- sorting and search that account for language, case and diacritics;
- language selection and persistence by the user, the default language.

If the project is single-language and multilingual support is not planned, assess in one short section only the formatting of dates, numbers and currencies and readiness for future translation.
