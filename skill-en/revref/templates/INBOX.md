# Incoming owner answers

Owner answers given outside the orchestrator's session are appended here: with the `/revref ask` command from a parallel session or manually. Append only.

At the next step boundary the orchestrator moves the answers to `DECISIONS.md` with the mark "answer from INBOX <date>" and deletes the moved entries from here (PROTOCOLS.md §7).
